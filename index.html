How we work

A studio, not a fund.

We don't just write checks — we co-found. Every company below started as a thesis inside Natis before it became its own team, brand, and product.

◆

Originate

We research new asset theories — novel models of ownership, value, and exchange — and turn the strongest ones into venture theses.

▲

Build

Our in-house design and engineering team ships a working product fast, validating the thesis with real users before scaling the team.

●

Spin out

Once a company has traction, we recruit an operating team, raise its own capital, and spin it out as an independent business.
