Mission

New theories of value deserve real companies.

Natis exists to take ideas about ownership, value, and exchange that are usually left to theory — and build them into products people actually use.

Our mission

Most valuable ideas about how assets should work — who owns what, how value is created, how exchange should happen — stay stuck as essays, papers, or side conversations. Natis's mission is to close that gap: to take the most promising of these theories and turn them into working startups, fast, with real users and real revenue.

We measure ourselves not by how many companies we start, but by how many theories we manage to prove — or disprove — in the real world.

What "new asset theory" means to us

It's a deliberately wide lens: new ways to define what counts as an asset, new mechanisms for owning or fractionalizing them, new markets for exchanging them, and new tools for pricing and valuing things that didn't have a market before. If a thesis changes how value moves between people, it's in scope.

Principles

How we hold ourselves to it

01

Theory must survive contact with users

An idea only counts once it's been tested against real people, real money, and real behavior — not just a model.

02

Speed is a form of rigor

Shipping fast is how we find out which theories are actually true. Slow validation is just a more expensive way to be wrong.

03

Ownership matters — including our own

We build companies designed to stand independently, with founders and teams who own real equity in what they build.

04

Small studio, sharp focus

We'd rather do a few things exceptionally well than spread thin across every trend in the space.
